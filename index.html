Shortcuts

Tap + Save to keep the current sounds and timer as a favourite. Long-press a favourite to rename or delete it. Long-press the Hush app icon to start Last mix, Favourite 1, White noise or Womb + heartbeat directly.

Safe use

Place the phone at least 2 m (7 ft) from the crib, never inside it. Keep volume low: at the crib it should sound quieter than soft conversation. A 2014 Pediatrics study found infant sound machines could exceed 85 dB up close, against a 50 dB nursery guideline. Prefer a timer over all-night sound.

Evidence

White noise has the clearest data: in a 1990 trial, 80% of newborns fell asleep within 5 minutes with it, against 25% without (Spencer et al.). Womb and heartbeat sounds and lullabies show calming effects in small studies, including in neonatal units. The other sounds work the same way as white noise, masking sudden noises, with little infant-specific data.
