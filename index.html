Safe use

Place the phone at least 2 m (7 ft) from the crib, never inside it. Keep volume low: at the crib it should sound quieter than soft conversation. A 2014 Pediatrics study found infant sound machines could exceed 85 dB up close, against a 50 dB nursery guideline. Prefer a timer over all-night sound.

Evidence

White noise has the clearest data: in a 1990 trial, 80% of newborns fell asleep within 5 minutes with it, against 25% without (Spencer et al.). Womb and heartbeat sounds show calming effects in small studies. The other sounds work the same way, masking sudden noises, with little infant-specific data. Effects usually fade after the first few months.
